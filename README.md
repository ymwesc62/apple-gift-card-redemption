## The code, the card, and the numbers that aren't the code

A physical card carries more printed data than most people expect: a barcode, sometimes a PIN, a retailer SKU, a serial number. Only one string adds money to an account. On Apple Gift Cards that string is 16 digits, and on many cards the final printing step is a label you peel or gently scratch to reveal it. Scraping with a coin edge is how people damage the digits they then can't read, so use a fingernail.

Emailed cards skip the typing problem entirely. Open the message and click **Redeem Now**. If someone forwarded you a screenshot instead of the original email, the button usually doesn't survive the screenshot, so ask for the original message or a clear photo of the 16 digits.

Cards aren't sold in every country, and a card bought in one region won't load onto an account registered in another. That single rule causes more failed redemptions than typos do, and no amount of retyping fixes it.

## Redeem it: iPhone, iPad, Mac, Windows, email

**iPhone, iPad, or Apple Vision Pro**

1. Open the App Store.
2. Tap your photo, your initials, or the sign-in icon in the top corner. You may be asked to sign in.
3. Tap **Redeem Code**.
4. Tap **Use Camera** and hold the card inside the frame. If the scan won't take, tap **Enter Code Manually** and type the 16 digits.
5. Tap **Redeem**.

On iPhone there's a second route in some countries and regions: add your Apple Account card to the Wallet app first, then open Wallet, tap that card, choose Redeem, and scan or type. It's the same balance landing in the same place, just a different door.

**Mac**

1. Open the App Store app.
2. Click your name in the bottom-left corner, or click Sign In if you aren't signed in.
3. Click **Redeem Gift Card** in the top-right corner.
4. Enter the code, or click **Use Camera**.

Camera redemption on a Mac only works when the card has a box printed around the code. Hold the card **4 to 7 inches (10 to 18 centimeters)** from the camera, keep the code area near the center of the preview window, and hold still until it reads. That distance range is the detail most guides leave out, and it's the difference between a scan that catches on the first try and one that keeps hunting.

**Windows PC**

1. Open the Apple Music app or the Apple TV app.
2. Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar, then click **Redeem**. Sign in first if prompted.
3. Follow the on-screen instructions, then click **Redeem**.

**In a browser**

Apple's support documentation points to its web redemption page, which works without installing anything. Sign in with the Apple Account you actually want credited, because the balance lands wherever you're signed in. Some third-party guides route people through the Media and Purchases section of the Apple Account page instead; Apple's own instructions name the redeem page, so start there.

## "Invalid code": the checklist, in the order to try it

1. **Type it instead of scanning it.** Glare, a curved card, or a worn print layer defeats the camera more often than the code itself is wrong. Manual entry removes the variable.
2. **Use the X code.** Apple's guidance is explicit: enter the redemption code beginning with X, not the other numbers on the card.
3. **Check the characters that look alike.** 0 and O, 1 and I, 8 and B, 5 and S. Photograph the code and zoom in rather than squinting at the card under a lamp.
4. **Strip stray spaces.** One third-party guide also states that codes are case-sensitive and include hyphens that must be typed exactly as printed. Apple's help page describes only a 16-digit code, so the two descriptions don't fully agree. If one form fails, try the other before concluding the card is dead.
5. **Stop retrying for 15 minutes.** Hammering the Redeem button with the same code can lock it temporarily. A third-party guide puts the wait at 15 minutes, which is cheap insurance either way.
6. **Consider the card's history.** Two things produce a real "invalid" result: the seller never activated the card, or someone already redeemed it. Bought at retail? Go back to the retailer with the receipt. Received as a gift? Ask the giver for the purchase confirmation, then contact Apple Support with it. In the US the gift card line is 1-800-275-2273.

A code that has already been redeemed is the worst case, because the balance is spent and Apple states it isn't responsible for unauthorized use. Which is why the next section matters more than any of the steps above.

## Expiry, account binding, and region locks

**The balance doesn't expire.** Retailer specifications list no expiration, with the download code dated 9999-12-31, and third-party documentation says the same. Spend it whenever.

**It's welded to one account.** Redeem into the wrong Apple Account and the balance can't be transferred, split between accounts, or converted to cash. This is the only irreversible step in the whole process, so check which account is signed in before you tap Redeem. Sharing a family? The balance doesn't move between members either.

**Region decides everything.** A US card loads onto a US Apple Account and is valid for US transactions only. A Canadian card can't be added to a US account. If you need the balance on an account in another country, you'd have to create an account in that region or change your existing account's region first.

**Checking what's left:** on iPhone, open the App Store, tap your profile icon, go to Media and Purchases, then View Account. The figure at the top is your Store Credit. On a Mac, App Store → your name → View Information → Store Credit.

## FAQ

**Does an Apple Gift Card expire?**
No. The balance sits in your account until you spend it, and there's no deadline to use it.

**Can I move the balance to a different Apple Account?**
No. Once redeemed, it belongs to that account. You can't split it, transfer it, or cash it out.

**Can I buy an iPhone or a Mac with the balance?**
Unclear, and you should treat it that way. Apple's marketing copy mentions Apple products and accessories; a third-party guide says hardware isn't covered. Apply the balance at checkout and see if it's accepted before you commit.

**Will a US card work on a UK or Canadian account?**
No. The card's country has to match the account's country or region.

**Can I get a refund if I redeemed the wrong card?**
Cards aren't returnable. For a mistaken redemption, contact Apple Support with proof of purchase; outcomes are decided case by case.

**What if the code still won't work after all the checks?**
Retail purchase goes back to the retailer with the receipt. A gift goes to Apple Support with the giver's purchase confirmation.
